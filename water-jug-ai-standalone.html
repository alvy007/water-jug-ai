    UNDER THE HOOD / 04

    The logic behind the search

    01

    Represent states

    We represent each state as (a, b), where a and b are the current amounts of water. The initial state is (0, 0).

    02

    Explore transitions

    For each state, the solver considers filling, emptying, and pouring water between the two jugs. Already-visited states are skipped.

    03

    Reach the goal

    The goal is reached when either jug holds the target amount. BFS guarantees a minimum-move solution; DFS finds a path without that guarantee.

    STATE-SPACE COMPLEXITY: AT MOST (CAPACITY A + 1) × (CAPACITY B + 1) UNIQUE STATES. TIME AND SPACE ARE O(A × B) FOR POSITIVE CAPACITIES.
